Biography

Eldar Həmidov was born in 2009 in Sumqayıt, Azerbaijan. He studied at the Sumqayıt İstedad Lyceum and now attends T. İsmayılov Secondary School No. 29.

He began competing in robotics in 2020, when his team placed second worldwide at the RoboCross Online Challenge in Egypt. Since then he has worked across robotics, AI and cybersecurity — reaching the finals of WRO and Teknofest, earning an Honor Roll at the International Olympiad in Artificial Intelligence, second place at the National Junior Cybersecurity Olympiad (NJCO 2025) and second place at AIRO 2026. In 2025, SASMO Azerbaijan announced him as a Silver award winner.

He runs EldarBuildLab, a YouTube channel where he documents robot builds and experiments, and volunteers with the Sumqayıt Youth House and Bir Könüllü. He works in Python and C++, and builds in Fusion 360, SolidWorks and FreeCAD.

He is currently finishing school, preparing for the next olympiad season, and studying machine learning.
